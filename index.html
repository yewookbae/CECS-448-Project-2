Get as close to 21 as you can.

Set a bet before the deal. Take turns choosing Hit or Stand. Number cards count as shown, face cards count as 10, and aces count as 1 or 11. The higher score at or below 21 wins; a tie returns the bet. Wins pay 1:1. Splitting and doubling are not part of this game.
